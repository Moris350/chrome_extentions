## Executive Summary

The AI Writing and Email Productivity space has **40M+ total installs** across top 10 players. Market valued at **$1.34B–$3.40B in 2026**, growing at **25%+ CAGR** to $20B+ by 2034.

**Key Finding**: Severe user backlash against incumbents (Grammarly, Wordtune, MaxAI) due to:
1. Browser bloat & typing lag
2. Aggressive paywalls ($12–$30/mo)
3. Privacy concerns (keystrokes sent to cloud)
4. Generic, robotic AI output
